
Under the Australian Taxation Office (ATO) rules:

- Depreciating assets are items that lose value over time, such as air conditioners, ovens, and appliances. You generally claim deductions for their decline in value over their effective life.
    
- Capital works are structural improvements to a property, such as buildings, extensions, and certain fences. Deductions are generally claimed at 2.5% per year over 40 years, subject to eligibility.
    
- Capital allowances is the broader tax term covering deductions for the decline in value of depreciating assets and deductions for capital works.
